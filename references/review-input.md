# Contribution review input

Use the installed `scripts/review_contribution.py` as the entry point. Give it the request file, then `--output public.json`; after obtaining the plan and household facts, run with `--final --output decision.json`. Both outputs rerun the same fixed model from its sources. Verify the exit code and read the newly created status and values.

The case includes ticker, model_id, market_date, facts_file, market_file and, for the final run, personal facts. Supply paths relative to the request or absolute paths. An initial public run can omit personal information. All financial data come from case-local observations of the issuer's original reports. Ask for missing personal information; leave regular_diversification absent when unknown.

## Fixed operating model

Select the registered model matching the issuer and the investment project. Follow the observed filing schema of the selected model. The industrial model schema and equity bridge are in [industrial-project.md](industrial-project.md). Include dated public source locators for each observation. The analyst identifies the documents and inputs published numbers. Only the installed model defines the growth and discount rules, useful lives, normalization, reinvestment treatment and full forecasts. The review runner rejects caller-provided forecasts, valuation endpoints, a target price, recommended contribution and decision narrative. When the fact packet cannot support the model, report the missing disclosure, affected business and implication.

Report the model identifier, version and fact digest. Show how reported segment earnings and central costs reconcile to the reported group figure in comparable periods. Identify each non-project business, retained/eliminated business, accounting adjustment and the difference between operating and ordinary profit. When the company discloses only pooled earnings, preserve the pool; report its linked components at the available precision. Identify which interval arises from conflicting or incomplete disclosures and which depends on a declared model convention. Do not present the endpoints as statistical confidence bounds. Show separately the cash needed for asset replacement, growth assets, and incremental operating working capital, with depreciation added back once. Match disclosures and project schedules to the same scope and period. Examine the return on incremental investment implied by terminal growth. Use both complete annual paths from construction to ramp-up and sustainable operation, reconcile project spending across years, and compare each close-date endpoint to the same completed close. Leave the fair value as a range. Break-even costs or thresholds are permitted when labeled and computed independently of a midpoint.

## Market facts and reproducibility

Market facts contain `market_date, target, peers, peer_exclusions, disclosure_check, guidance_url, project_url`. For each issuer supply ticker, overlap, product_url, close, close_date, close_url, eps, filing_date, fiscal_year_end, filing_url, site_pe, site_date and site_url. Use latest issuer full-year EPS published by that close, with common per-share currency and split basis. Recheck the dated website P/E; document the observed site basis or sources tried when unavailable. Record named exclusions and evidence for them. Compare later corrected observations to the original with source and publication date.

For two outputs of this entry point, optional `previous_decision_file` compares matching issuers and closing sessions and records changes in the model version, observations and endpoint values. For older point-estimate files, retain the original record, identify its operating assumptions and explain its position relative to the current interval. Neither an archived model nor a discretionary midpoint supplies the decision value.

## Final answer

Use `decision_fair_value_range_per_share` and the interval/cost relationship in `contribution_comparison`. Show the actual before-tax incentive-adjusted cost, source-based peer comparison, and annual cash/reward difference. Describe any estimated dividend dates and tax uncertainty. The installed contribution policy compares the current monthly amount with a one-third reduction in whole yen; confirm eligibility before presenting it as an available election. Its 10% concentration review threshold is a declared decision policy, independent of issuer forecasts. If the adjusted cost lies within the interval, affordability, employment exposure and the user's regular diversification govern the comparison. Where regular diversification is unknown, explain the conditional outcome. Keep the explanation accessible; include the path to the full decision and annual workings.
