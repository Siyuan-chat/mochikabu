# Mapping financial statements to observations

Start with the latest consolidated annual filings, interim earnings release, capex announcement and corrections known by the regular-session close. Use issuer or exchange originals. Supply a ledger of source URL, page or named table, original row label, selected column header, accounting scope, units, period start/end and publication date. Preserve signs, including losses. Identify splits, acquisitions, disposals, restatements, changes of fiscal year and consolidation changes before comparing periods.

Read table headings; use the latest current-year forecast, distinct from prior guidance, year-to-date figures, ordinary profit, adjusted earnings and profit attributable to owners. Use the most recent comparative on the same accounting basis. Where a PDF's text reading order interleaves columns, consult the original page layout and corroborate with a numerical statement or another table in the filing. Record EPS and operating profit ranges as ranges. Apply unit scaling, component arithmetic, normalization, forecast and decision rules only through the selected script.

Use the last three complete consecutive annual periods for sales, EBIT, depreciation and cash purchases of productive assets. Cash paid for property/equipment and intangibles belongs to investing cash flow. If PPE and intangibles share a single row, count it once. Never replace cash purchases with gross additions, net investing cash flow or total investment including acquisitions. For working capital map trade receivables including contract assets when combined, inventories and trade payables; exclude other financial balances unless the issuer reports an inseparable combined trade line. Retain the original definition.

Use the latest interim or annual balance sheet available by the close for the equity bridge unless the selected model requires a dated fiscal-end forecast. Identify interest-bearing debt and lease liabilities, noncontrolling interests, shares issued and treasury shares, split basis and dividend entitlement. State missing data and effects. Never infer a precise financial line by dividing price by a rounded trading multiple.

For expansions identify the consolidated economic owner, approval date, scheduled construction and operating dates, disclosed total investment, scope and cumulative spend when available. Identify overlap with group capital spending. Disclose operating capacity, operating profit targets and product segment controls only where published. An increase in capacity is not an equal increase in sales or profit.

For comparable multiples use independent listed producers with demonstrable direct product overlap and comparable year-end issuer EPS. Check industry membership and product pages. List the qualified domestic closest peers before using diversified or foreign references. Apply the same same-session quote and EPS-source hierarchy to peers and target. Prefer the dated historical valuation row for the displayed multiple; where a vendor uses different earnings, keep the displayed value and its basis separate. Record specific exclusions. Audit claims against the source rather than seeking agreement by sharing populated facts.
