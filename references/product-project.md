# Product earnings milestone model

Select `expansion_fcff_v1` when the issuer discloses product operating-profit multiples with a base year and target years, annual product sales, comparable segment and group profits, and a project budget separating land, buildings and equipment. Two operating-margin peers and the separate valuation P/E peer exercise serve different purposes. A capacity multiple, unit shipment target or consolidated sales aspiration does not qualify as a product earnings target.

Submit a JSON file containing exactly `model,ticker,market_date,tables,product_tables,project,product`. Both arrays use the labelled-table format in [the industrial project contract](industrial-project.md). A single record has one scope, period, filing date, original column, unit, URL and table/page. Each row carries its original label and signed value. All company observations use the issuer’s fiscal year-end date, translated from the issuer’s own fiscal calendar. Different-currency peers keep their own fiscal calendar and currency.

|Input|Observations|
|---|---|
|`tables` annual history|Three consecutive fiscal years of consolidated revenue, reported operating profit, operating cash-flow depreciation, cash purchases of fixed assets, and year-end trade receivables, inventories and payables. Follow the historical fields and units in the industrial contract.|
|`tables` current|Consolidated full-year `forecast` point values for sales, ebit, da. Latest actual date for issued and treasury share counts, in a common split basis. Project scope, kind plan: `project_budget` at the ramp-completion fiscal year-end.|
|`product`|`pool` is the name of the disclosed segment containing the product. `base_fy_end` is the closing date of the published earnings target's base period. `dividend_record_date` belongs to the forward dividend, using the same share basis. Optional `disposed_segment` names the affected non-pool segment.|
|`project`|`operation_fy_end` and `ramp_fy_end` identify the fiscal years of commissioning and the final capacity/earnings milestone. The instrument models construction in the years between current annual guidance and commissioning. Record the issuer’s dates and any difference between the model’s spending schedule and announced timing.|

Populate `product_tables` as follows. Currency fields use the published JPY unit, except where noted. Supply every segment and consolidation adjustment from a comparable presentation for each selected annual period. In particular, the guidance column is a full-year forecast.

|scope|kind and field|Source meaning|
|---|---|---|
|group and segment/<issuer label>|actual and forecast, normalized_ebit|Consolidated control total and corresponding segment profits, using one set of adjustments and the same organizational structure across periods.|
|group|actual, roic|Published annual return on invested capital, unit percent, in each historical fiscal year.|
|group|forecast, net_debt and dividend|Forecast fiscal year-end net interest-bearing debt and per-share dividend at the record date, unit JPY/share for dividend.|
|group|actual and plan, maintenance|Realized and planned maintenance and renewal investment. Supply total and exact first and last dates of each multi-year period.|
|group|actual, disposed_sales and disposed_ebit|When the base includes a disposal, its base-year external revenue and signed operating profit with the company’s comparable segment mapping.|
|product|actual, sales|Revenue of the identified product in the target’s base year.|
|product|plan, profit_multiple|One record per issuer-published target, unit multiple. A 2× target is entered as 2.|
|project|plan, land, building, equipment|Budget components at the ramp-completion fiscal end. The script reconciles them to the announced total.|
|peer/<distinct issuer>|actual, sales and ebit|Full-year operating revenue and profit for the same comparable business scope. Specify the original currency and scale, such as JPY million or TWD thousand. The ratio is dimensionless.|

The engine brackets the undisclosed product base profit using the observed peer margins, keeps the non-product segment group together by observed year, and models the published product profit target as the high outcome. The low case holds base product profit flat despite investment. The numerical policy in the output records how maintenance, depreciation, growth reinvestment and construction spending are phased. Identify proxy profits and scheduling assumptions in the user-facing explanation. Use the sustained interval without a midpoint. Publish both current-close DCF endpoints and the separately recomputed market comparison through `review_contribution.py`.

## Freshness and peer selection for live runs
Open the issuer's medium-term plan archive, subsequent annual strategy/IR day updates and any revisions through the close. For planned maintenance locate the latest cash-allocation slide; where original/previous and revised/current coexist, take current. On that group plan table include revision_check={"checked_through":"YYYY-MM-DD","index_url":"https://...","latest_publication":"YYYY-MM-DD","locator":"latest applicable plan document, page and current column"}. Record original, revised and dates in the ledger. For realized prior maintenance keep the reported actual.

Market P/E peers must manufacture the same target product for the same end application, evidenced by their own product page. Exclude upstream materials, distributors and adjacent electronic materials even when they share a sector. When screening foil distinguish current-collector battery grades, flexible-circuit rolled foil and electrodeposited circuit-board foil; record actual application overlap. For operating-margin proxies first search listed specialist manufacturers of the target product. Use the latest complete annual financials available by the close, original currency and units; prefer a separately disclosed same-product operating segment, otherwise a specialist's operating statements; identify mixed grades and accounting differences. Use issuer tickers as peer identifiers; use the target's full precision annual product sales note, not rounded slide charts. Use Kabutan's historical valuation row labelled with the exact close date as first website cross-check.

For disposals read both transferred revenue and signed operating profit in the same annual financial statement business separation / discontinued operations note. Use its original precision and common source, note locator, column and units for both rows. A rounded slide is insufficient. When screening market multiples, require the same manufacturing stage: an electrodeposition producer competes with other producers of original electrodeposited foil. Firms that only purchase, etch, thin, laminate or otherwise convert original foil belong in exclusions. Include comparable original-foil producers even when the rest of their group differs. Use exact dated historical site multiples and full precision audited amounts for all affected rows, including the divested business.

## Segment-period completeness
Mandatory segment_coverage in financial.json for this model. Inspect the latest issuer presentation’s LONGEST comparable annual trend chart and its legend across both actuals and latest guidance, including the organization-change slide, bridge panels and recast annual comparatives; the quarterly detail table alone is insufficient. For each of the same three historical consolidated year ends and current forecast, record exactly one column. Use all full years in this window on the same normalized operating profit definition and new organizational basis. A year explicitly restated in any chart/appendix must be included. Prior-basis-only years need an exclusion reason, basis and checked filing URLs; no reconstructed or estimated allocations. Exclude superseded forecasts and ordinary profit. Confirm values visually where chart text-order is ambiguous. In this model the full annual comparison is the control for the separate product_tables observations, and their values must agree.

Capture all scopes in each comparable column, including negative/zero amounts and consolidation adjustments, using the full legend independently of the supplied model rows. Use consistent segment scope identifiers across charts (for a consolidation adjustment, use the canonical full label from the detailed table, e.g. 調整額 when chart shortens it to 調整). For historical working capital take only the required model components. For proxies use primary filed annual statements at full precision.

For historical group ROIC compare the consolidated ROE/ROIC time-series in the financial KPI section to the portfolio/segment overview. Use the most precise published realized percentages for the same scope; inspect the actual data labels, not axis ticks or integers from the strategy overview. Document both source locators if they differ in display precision. Close prices must be the unrounded regular-session closing trade from the exchange daily quotation sheet (afternoon close, check ticker); use full decimal ticks and cross-check a second dated historical OHLC row. Retrieve website P/E from the exact dated historical valuation row (Kabutan daily, weekly/monthly only if same row date), not a rounded live quote header. Verify the price in that valuation row against the exchange close before accepting the displayed multiple.
