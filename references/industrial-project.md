# Industrial project FCFF model
Use model ID `industrial_project_fcff_v1` for a consolidated Japanese industrial issuer with a disclosed plant investment amount and construction/commissioning dates but without a supportable project profit path. Apply the other registered model where the issuer discloses the product earnings targets and that model's complete input set. Financials and market quotes are independent files in the case directory.

## Financial file

Top-level fields: `model, ticker, market_date, tables, project`, optional `notes`. Project requires `name, start_fy_end, operation_fy_end`: dates are ISO closing dates of the **issuer's** fiscal years containing construction start and targeted commissioning. When the issuer specifies a month also record start_month and operation_month in YYYY-MM; the runner checks their fiscal-year mapping and whether construction is still in the future. Interpret fiscal-year names against the issuer's calendar. Map a calendar month in May to the fiscal year actually containing that May. Start with the most recent material issuer-announced, costed expansion; for multiple projects include only projects valued by the selected model and disclose excluded projects. This version supports one identified project.

Each table has the following format. Repeat a table when a unit, scope, reporting status or source changes. The numbers in a table are from the stated row/column intersection. Do not select cells by page coordinates. Use ISO dates, public sources available before the closing auction, and fiscal periods rather than PDF title years. Same-day revisions released after the market closes become eligible at the next close.

```json
{"source_url":"https://issuer.example/filing.pdf","published":"2025-05-12","page":"consolidated cash flow p.8","scope":"consolidated","period_start":"2024-04-01","period_end":"2025-03-31","kind":"actual","column":"当連結会計年度","unit":"JPY million","rows":{"ppe_cash":["有形固定資産の取得による支出",-10]}}
```

A row value is either a labelled cell or a list of distinct component cells (e.g. current and noncurrent debt). Keep signs/units as reported; cash asset purchases are converted to positive outlays. Use separate rows even when their issuer labels differ from these semantic keys. For balance sheets and shares `period_start` may equal `period_end`; for full-year forecasts/flows supply the fiscal year's genuine start and end. `kind` is actual, forecast or plan. Tables of project observations have `scope=project`; all operating/financing tables have `scope=consolidated`. Supported units are JPY/JPY thousand/JPY million/JPY 100 million/JPY billion, or shares/thousand shares/million shares. Where the same semantic observation appears more than once, the newest eligible restatement controls; inconsistent duplicates in one publication are rejected.

## Fixed conventions
- Anchor DCF directly at the regular closing session. First period contains only the remaining fiscal-year fraction; projected annual operating profit, D&A and recurring capex are prorated by actual days. Discount future fiscal-end FCFF at 8%. Cash tax convention is 30% on positive operating profit. Use the most recent balance sheet to bridge EV to common equity, with a visible as-of date. Shares use the same post-action basis as the price.
- Start from company guidance. Hold sales flat after the guided year. Group margins fade over three years to the lower/higher of the relevant guidance margin and prior three annual margins. These are group operating scenarios. Project-specific unit profit or utilization uplift is not imputed without disclosure.
- Spread remaining project cost evenly over the remaining construction fiscal-year equivalents, including the commissioning year. When construction is already under way and spend-to-date is undisclosed, combined scenarios include full outstanding budget or zero remaining; identify this uncertainty. When construction has not started, the full budget is outstanding in both scenarios. Count first-year cash capex guidance only to the extent it exceeds the already modeled project and maintenance cash flows.
- Legacy D&A and replacement come from historical ratios to revenue. Stress replacement also tests historical total cash capex, which can contain other growth spend. New plant assets enter the replacement and D&A base over three operating years using a fixed composite 20-year life. Use historical operating working-capital intensity applied to incremental revenue. Terminal growth is zero: undisclosed positive growth and its funding are not capitalized. Extend the explicit schedule past commissioning before applying the perpetuity. No statistical probability or unsupported midpoint is assigned.
- If the project economics are not separately disclosed, report the range as conditional on consolidated margins and the stated investment, and identify that incremental plant earnings and any volume uplift from the new capacity are unmeasured. Historical consolidated data must be comparable; material disposals/restarts, non-operating holdings and accounting changes can render this model inappropriate.

## Required discovery checks (industrial policy 1.1)
Include financial.json.guidance_check with keys da and capex_cash. Each value is {"status":"reported","source_url":"https://...","locator":"page, table and basis"}. Open the latest issuer supplement, financial data book and annual KPI forecast table, not just the cover. Search the current full-year plan for depreciation/amortization and investment. If available on the same accounting basis, status must be reported and the forecast row must be recorded. Otherwise use not_disclosed or different_basis and cite the specific table inspected and the scope mismatch. A management capex plan without evidence it is cash paid is different_basis.

For the consolidated industrial P/E median screen exchange-listed, independent manufacturers of the **same principal end product as the expansion**, across geographies. Search Japanese product associations and competitor disclosures. Require actual same-day trades, latest company EPS for the same fiscal end and disclosed sales of the same product family (not a broader adjacent category). Include all eligible candidates you find. At least half of consolidated external revenue in the latest completed full year must be attributable to that product family. Sum geographical segment external sales if necessary. In each eligible market.json peers row add core_sales = {"product_sales":123,"group_sales":200,"source_url":"https://...","locator":"original revenue table, currency, unit, year and lines"}. Record mixed businesses, adjacent products, missing quantitative segments and non-trading companies in peer_exclusions. The 50% cutoff is a fixed comparability screen, not a claim made by the company. A one-peer cross-check is labelled as such. Use Kabutan historical valuation row for the exact trading session first; prefer daily then weekly or monthly only if the displayed row bears the exact session date. Use another dated historical provider only when that source gives no value. Do not take a live quote-card P/E.
