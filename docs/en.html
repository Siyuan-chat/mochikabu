FAQ

Common questions

Is Mochikabu an ESOP or ESPP calculator?

Its primary target is Japanese employee stock ownership and employee shareholding arrangements such as 社員持株会 and 従業員持株会. It does not assume US ESOP or ESPP rules are equivalent.

Does a high incentive automatically mean increase contributions?

No. Incentives reduce effective acquisition cost, but valuation, affordability, concentration, lockups and contribution windows still matter.

Does Mochikabu execute trades?

No. It supports research, valuation, scenario analysis and contribution decisions, but does not place orders.
